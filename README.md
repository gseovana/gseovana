## 👋 About

I'm a **M.Sc. student in Computer Science** at the **Federal University of Ouro Preto (UFOP)**, Brazil.

My research lies at the intersection of **Machine Learning**, **Natural Language Processing**, and **Computational Social Science**, with a focus on understanding human behavior and information dynamics through large-scale online data.

I enjoy developing reproducible machine learning pipelines, analyzing social networks, and building computational methods for studying digital platforms.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Computational Social Science
- Social Network Analysis
- Semi-supervised Learning
- Reproducible Artificial Intelligence
